All example values in fixtures are synthetic. Every field below is a **normalized** field in the application's ledger/model; the *Source(s)* column names the vendor field(s) it is derived from with the S-ID of the research entry (`research/public_sources_apis.md`, `research/public_sources_sellerboard.md`), `OBS` for the authorized audit observations, `PROP` for proposed contracts, `CD-nn` for cookbook discovery items (cookbook §0.12; cross-referenced there to `integration/connector_contracts.md` DISC-01..27).

Conventions: `money` = `NUMERIC(18,4)` (storage precision; wire format string decimal 4 dp; presentation HALF_EVEN 2 dp; floors CEILING to minor unit) exact Decimal in source currency with a sibling `*_ccy` (ISO-4217) and a derived `*_rep` (reporting currency) computed by CALC-026; `ratio` = `numeric(12,8)`; `ts` = UTC timestamp with time zone; `date` = calendar date in the store time zone unless stated. PII: **Y** = personal data under Shopify protected-customer-data (S24) or Amazon PII/restricted roles (S27/S29); **H** = stored only as a salted hash; **N** = none. Retention: `LEDGER` = life of workspace (raw and derived financial facts, min 8 years for Indian books — configurable per F-A08, observed Sellerboard default 18 months / up to 60); `PII-30d` = Amazon PII deleted ≤30 days after delivery (S27 Extra); `PII-SHOP` = Shopify protected data, purpose-limited, erased on `customers/redact` / `shop/redact` webhooks within 30 days (S24); `REPORT-90d` = raw report documents kept 90 days then only the parsed rows (S30); `OPS-24m` = operational/analytic data 24 months rolling; `CONFIG` = versioned configuration kept for the life of the workspace.

## 9. Field-level notes

- **Never stored**: buyer names, raw e-mail addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, PAN numbers of individuals, card or bank details, Amazon buyer IDs. *(v0.2 change, PROPOSED for owner approval)*: the **GSTIN of B2B business customers and of own entities** is stored (`b2b_customer.gstin`, `entity.gstin`) because place of supply, Rule 46 invoice fields and dedup need it; it is a business identifier, access-restricted to `finance`, `sales` (own customers), `owner`, `admin`, and masked in exports. Customer contact persons are stored only as a role label and business e-mail if the owner approves (default off). Where a calculation needs identity (LTV, MCF matching) only salted hashes (`buyer_key`, `ship_postcode_hash`) are kept, keyed per market, with the hash version recorded so a re-salt is auditable.
- **Amazon PII windows**: any restricted-role field (delivery address components) is used transiently to derive `ship_zone` and discarded within the 30-day limit (S27 Extra); the derived zone is not PII.
- **Shopify protected data**: `customer.email` is read only if the L1/L2 approval is granted; otherwise `buyer_key` is `NULL` and customer calcs report `IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE`. GDPR webhooks (`customers/redact`, `shop/redact`) delete `customer`, `subscription_contract` and re-key cohorts within 30 days (S24).
- **Report documents**: raw settlement, ledger, returns, reimbursement and traffic documents are stored as received for 90 days (REPORT-90d) for replay; the parsed rows live in the ledger permanently. Settlement reports are system-generated and must be captured on schedule (S30).
- **Unknown fields**: every CD-nn reference above (cookbook §0.12, mapped to connector DISC-nn there) is a field whose vendor name or semantics is not confirmed by a rendered official page at cutoff; the normalized field is fixed, the mapping is to be completed in WP-01/WP-05 and recorded in the fee taxonomy / connector mapping tables.
- **Region data (rev. 2026-10-10)**: only a derived region code is stored for the Map view. For Amazon, the ship-to state is read only when the Restricted Data Token role is approved (`region_setting.amazon_rdt_approved`); without it the region is taken from a region-level report or set to `UNKNOWN` (CALC-111).
- **Marketplaces and channels (v0.3)**: adding an Amazon marketplace or a channel market is a `marketplace_master` row plus fixtures, not a code change (REQ-189). Tax regimes are effective-dated in `marketplace_tax_regime_history` (valid_from / valid_to, canonical enum `VAT_SELLER | GST_SELLER | CONSUMPTION_TAX | SALES_TAX_MF | MF_VAT`); a change never rewrites a closed period. *(v0.3 rev 2)* Regimes and fee-code maps of every non-IN marketplace are PROPOSED / UNVERIFIED (only Amazon.in and Shopify India were audited). Seller establishment / OSS, ship-from country, Amazon Business reverse charge and stock-transfer VAT are recorded as flags for the CA only (⚠, A-22). Walmart / eBay / TikTok Shop buyer data is not stored beyond the identifiers above (no names, addresses or e-mails).
- **Marketing fields (v0.3)**: rows marked OMITTED v0.3 are not created in the schema and no connector requests the scopes behind them (REQ-188).
- **B2B documents (v0.2)**: posted invoices, credit notes, debit notes and receipts are immutable LEDGER records kept at least 8 years (Indian books); corrections are new documents. Rates, thresholds and periods marked ⚠ live in §10.3 masters with effective dates and a `verified_by` field for the CA sign-off.
